# Call-first outreach (businesses that only list a phone)

Most local businesses only publish a phone number. So the sequence is:

**Call → get the decision-maker's name + email → send the email the same day → follow up.**

An email they *agreed to receive* lands in the inbox, gets opened, and is
CAN-SPAM-clean. It turns cold outreach into a warm follow-up.

## The sequence (per business)
| Day | Touch | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Call** (10-11:30 AM or 2-4 PM; avoid lunch rush and Mondays) | Reach the owner/manager, or get their name + email from the front desk |
| 0 | **Email** (only if they gave an address) | "As promised on the phone…": the B1/B3 email, first line changed to reference the call |
| 3 | **Email follow-up 1** (value) | Hard-water tip or storm/gutter tip |
| 5 | **Call back** if no reply | "Did you get a chance to look at my email?" |
| 7 | **Email follow-up 2** (proof + real urgency) | Last email |
| 10+ | **Walk in** with a card if it's nearby | Face to face beats everything |

## Call script: front desk (most calls)
> "Hi, this is Santana with Flair Cleaning Co, we're local here in Granite Bay.
> Quick question: who takes care of the windows and outside of the building for you, is that the owner or an office manager?"

- **If they name someone:** "Great, what's the best email for [name]? I'll send over a short note so they can look at it when they have a minute."
- **"Just tell me what it's about":** "We're opening our first business route on Douglas Blvd: front glass on a set schedule, before you open, with photos after each visit. I'd love to send [name] a two-minute email about it."
- **"We already have someone":** "Totally fine. A lot of offices keep us as a backup for when their regular guy doesn't show. Could I send my info just in case?"
- **"Not interested":** "No problem at all, thanks for your time. Have a good one." (Mark *Lost / no thanks*; don't call again.)

## Call script: decision-maker
> "Hi [name], this is Santana, I own Flair Cleaning Co here in Granite Bay. I'll be quick.
> We've cleaned 100+ homes in town and we're opening our first business route on [street]. I'm saving five founding spots at a rate we lock for a year.
> Would it be worth me stopping by for five minutes to look at your windows and give you a price?"

- **Yes:** book a day/time on the call. Log as *Meeting / quote*.
- **"Send me something":** "Sure, what's the best email?" → send the email today.
- **"How much?":** "Depends on the glass. Most storefronts on [street] run about $[price] a visit. I'd rather see it and give you an exact number." *(Fill in once pricing is set.)*

## Voicemail (20 seconds, leave it once)
> "Hi, this is Santana with Flair Cleaning Co in Granite Bay. We're opening a business route on [street] for windows and gutters, with photos after every visit. I'd love to give you a quick price. My number is 916-234-8343. Again, 916-234-8343. Thanks!"

## Email after the call (replace paragraph 1 of B1/B3)
**Subject:** Following up on our call, [Name]
> [Name], thanks for taking my call today [or: "your front desk said you're the right person"].
> As promised, here's the short version: [B1 or B3 body + CTA, unchanged].

## Rules
- Calls are fine to businesses (no TCPA issue for live calls to a business line). **Never text** a business that hasn't texted us first.
- Log every call in the CRM's Outreach Log: Channel = *Call*, Outcome = *Talked in person / No answer / Meeting booked*.
- 15 to 25 calls per session; batch them. Most calls take under 2 minutes.
