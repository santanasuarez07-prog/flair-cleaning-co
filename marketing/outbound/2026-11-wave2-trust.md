# Outbound Wave 2: Trust-first cold emails (Granite Bay first, then the area)

**Principle:** people say yes when they *trust* they'll get something
valuable back. Every email earns trust in this order:

1. **Relevance:** "I'm local, and I noticed *your* building/storefront/community."
2. **Confidence:** short and direct. Name the result we deliver, no hedging.
3. **Proof:** a real testimonial plus a real mini case study.
4. **Low risk:** a free walkthrough, one free test clean, or one building first.
5. **One easy ask:** a yes/no question they can answer in 5 seconds.

**Nothing has been sent.** Granite Bay goes first; Rocklin, Loomis, Folsom,
El Dorado Hills and Auburn follow once we see what Granite Bay replies to.
