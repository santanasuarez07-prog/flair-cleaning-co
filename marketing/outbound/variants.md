# Cold email variants: Honest Pro voice

All variants use Angle B ("Honest Pro"): owner-run, 100+ local homes, real 5★
reviews, clear process. **No claims of existing business clients.**
Each variant changes **one thing** vs. the champion, so the data shows what won.

Footer on every email (unchanged):
```
Santana Suarez
Owner, Flair Cleaning Co
(916) 234-8343 | flaircleaningcompany.com
8687 Willow Valley Place, Granite Bay, CA 95746
Not a fit? Reply "no thanks" and I won't email again.
```

## B1. Founding 5 (CHAMPION, week 1; v1 text below)
**What it tests:** scarcity + status (be one of the first 5)
**Subject:** Founding spot on our Douglas Blvd route, [Name]
> [Name],
>
> I'm Santana Suarez, owner of Flair Cleaning Co. We've cleaned windows,
> gutters and solar on 100+ homes around Granite Bay, and we're now opening
> our first business route along [street].
>
> I'm taking 5 founding [offices/businesses]:
> - Glass, frames and tracks on a set schedule, before you open
> - A text the day before, photos after every visit
> - Founding rate locked for 12 months
>
> Want one of the spots?

## B2. The Audition (on the bench, not in the week 1 test)
**What it tests:** the **offer** (a free first clean instead of a locked rate)
**Subject:** Let us audition for [Business]
> [Name],
>
> I'm Santana Suarez, owner of Flair Cleaning Co. We've cleaned 100+ homes
> around Granite Bay, and we're opening our first business route on [street].
>
> Rather than ask you to take my word for it, let us audition: **we'll clean
> your front glass once at no charge.** If it's not the best your storefront
> has looked, you've lost nothing. If it is, we'll keep it that way on a set
> schedule, with a text the day before and photos after every visit.
>
> Want to pick a morning?

*Cost check: a free front-glass clean is about 20 to 30 minutes of a tech's time. Only offer
it if you're happy to give it.*

| 4 (EDH + Auburn) | winner wk3 | subject-line test | subject | 25 / 25 |

**How we call a winner:** after 25+ sends each, the higher **reply rate** wins
(positive replies break ties). If both are under 2% replies, both lose: write
two new variants. Retire the loser and record it in `../experiments.md`.
