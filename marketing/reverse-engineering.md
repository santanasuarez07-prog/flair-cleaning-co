# Reverse-engineering marketing that works

Whenever Santana shares an ad, reel, email, flyer or competitor's page, Claude
breaks it down with this sheet, adapts it into Flair outreach, and tests it
against the current champion. Never copy word for word: steal the *structure*,
rewrite the words in Flair's voice, keep every claim true.

## Step 1: Break it down (fill every line)
| Part | Question | Example answer |
|---|---|---|
| **Who it's for** | Who exactly is this talking to? | Homeowners with solar, 40+, suburban |
| **Hook (first 2 s / first line)** | What stops the scroll or gets the open? | "Your solar panels are losing you money" |
| **Emotional pull** | Fear, pride, greed, curiosity, belonging, convenience? | loss aversion |
| **Angle / story** | Neighbor, pro/status, best results, underdog, insider? | "I'm the guy your neighbor hired" |
| **Proof** | How does it earn trust? (before/after, numbers, reviews, demo, face) | split-screen before/after |
| **Offer** | What do they get, and why now? | free inspection, this week only |
| **CTA** | What's the one action, and how easy? | "comment CLEAN" / "reply YES" |
| **Format** | Length, pacing, on-screen text, music, who's on camera | 15 s, owner on camera, text overlay |
| **Why it works** | One sentence | Shows the problem before naming the price |
| **What NOT to copy** | Anything fake, pushy, off-brand or illegal for us | fake scarcity, cold texting |

## Step 2: Adapt it to each Flair channel
Write one version per channel, all using the same hook and angle:
- **Customer text** (≤ 160 chars, "Reply YES", Flair named in the first line)
- **Cold email to businesses** (Honest Pro voice, 60 to 90 words, small ask)
- **Phone opener** (one sentence to the front desk)
- **Reel/post for Flair's own Instagram or Facebook** (Higgsfield can produce it from real job photos)

## Step 3: Test it
- Log it in `experiments.md` as a challenger against the current champion. Change ONE thing.
- Customer texts: compare reply rate and bookings after 14 days.
- Cold email: 25+ sends each before calling a winner (`outbound/cold-email-tests.csv`).

## Step 4: Keep the swipe file
Save every breakdown in `swipes/YYYY-MM-DD-<source>.md`, winners and losers. Over time,
the patterns that keep working for Flair go into `playbook.md`.

## Patterns that reliably work for local exterior cleaning (starting library)
These are well-known patterns in the industry. Each still has to prove itself on Flair's own numbers.
1. **"Just finished on your street."** Proof the crew is nearby plus social proof. Text past customers near today's job; door hangers on the 5 houses on each side.
2. **Before/after split.** One photo, no words needed. Strongest on Facebook/Instagram and in an email *after* a reply.
3. **Problem you can't unsee.** "Look at the bottom of your windows right now." It gets the reader to check, and then they notice.
4. **Seasonal deadline.** Storms, pollen, holidays, listing season: a real reason to act now.
5. **Risk reversal.** "If it's not the cleanest your windows have been, you don't pay." Only if the owners will honor it.
6. **Owner on camera.** A 15 s phone video of Santana talking to the camera builds more trust than any polished ad.
7. **Curiosity ask.** "I took a photo of your storefront, want me to send it?" (cold email F1/F4).
8. **Bundle and one visit.** "Gutters + windows + driveway in one visit." Bigger tickets, one trip.
