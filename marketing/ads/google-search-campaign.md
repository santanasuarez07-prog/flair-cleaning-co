# Google Search campaign: Holiday Lights + Gutters ($200 test)

Goal: phone calls from people searching **right now** in Granite Bay, Roseville, Rocklin,
Loomis, Folsom, El Dorado Hills and Lincoln. Budget: **$200 over ~14 days** (Nov 23 to Dec 6).
Why Search (not Local Services Ads): LSA needs a background check and license/insurance
verification that takes 1 to 3 weeks; too slow for holiday lights. Start LSA in parallel for spring.

## Step-by-step setup (ads.google.com, signed in as contact@flaircleaningcompany.com)
1. **New campaign → goal "Leads" → type "Search".** Conversion goal: "Phone call leads".
2. **Switch to Expert Mode** if Google pushes "Smart campaign" (Smart campaigns waste small budgets).
3. **Networks:** uncheck **Search Partners** and **Display Network**.
4. **Locations:** add Granite Bay, Roseville, Rocklin, Loomis, Folsom, El Dorado Hills, Lincoln (CA).
   Location options → **"Presence: people in or regularly in your locations"** (not "interest").
5. **Language:** English. **Audience segments:** skip.
6. **Budget:** **$14/day**. (Google can spend up to 2x on a single day but averages out to the daily budget.)
   **Set a calendar reminder to pause it on Dec 6.**
7. **Bidding:** **Maximize clicks**, with **max CPC limit $6**. (New account, no conversion data yet;
   after 15 or more conversions, switch to Maximize conversions.)
8. **Ad schedule:** Mon to Sat, 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM. **Only when someone can answer the phone.**
9. **Ad groups:** create the two below. Put **~65% of attention on Holiday Lights** (its season ends Dec 10).
   If Holiday Lights spends everything, that's fine; it's the time-sensitive one.
10. **Ad:** one Responsive Search Ad per group. Final URL = **https://www.flaircleaningcompany.com**
    (check the page shows the phone number at the top and mentions holiday lights + gutters).
11. **Assets:** **Call asset (916) 234-8343** with call reporting ON. **Location asset**: link your Google
    Business Profile. Sitelinks optional.
12. **Negative keywords** (campaign level, below).
13. **Conversions:** Tools → Conversions → "Calls from ads", **count calls ≥ 60 seconds**.
14. **Launch**, then check after 24 to 48 hours (Search terms report: add bad searches as negatives).

## Ad groups and copy (all within Google's limits: headline ≤ 30, description ≤ 90)
See `adgroups.md` (generated by `build_ad.py`). Pin **"Call Flair Cleaning Co"** nowhere; let Google rotate.
**Before launching:** remove "5-Star Rated Local Crew" / "5-star rated" lines if your Google
Business Profile rating is not 5.0. Only claim what Google shows.

## What to expect (honest)
- Local CPCs for these terms usually run about **$3 to $10 per click**, so $200 ≈ **20 to 50 clicks**.
- With calls answered fast, that's typically **3 to 8 real leads**. One or two holiday light jobs pay for the test.
- The ad only works if the phone gets answered. **Missed calls = wasted money.** Turn on Flyra's
  call forwarding / missed-call text-back if available.

## Tracking (Claude reports this in the daily report)
- Google Ads: clicks, calls ≥ 60 s, cost per call (Santana reads them out or shares a screenshot;
  Claude can't log into Google Ads from here).
- Flyra: set lead source **"Google Ads"** on every lead/customer that came from the ad
  (Settings → Lead sources: create "Google Ads"), so revenue per dollar is measurable.
- Win = cost per booked job **< $60** for gutters, **< $100** for holiday lights.
